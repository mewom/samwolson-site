Sam Wolson

Sam Wolson is a visual storyteller whose works at the intersection of documentary and emerging technology. He currently serves as the Visual Features Editor at The New Yorker, an advisor at Agog, and a director, photographer, and journalist. His projects have premiered at major festivals including Sundance, Venice, and SXSW. Notable credits include co-directing We Who Remain, the first character-driven VR film shot in an active warzone. His immersive project Reeducated earned both a Peabody Award and an Emmy, and his interactive documentary Cleared by Fire won a second Peabody Award in 2026.
