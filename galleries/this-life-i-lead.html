This Life I Lead

Long-form photo essay following Shanon Fulcher as he navigates the ups and downs of life in Oakland California over three years. The project explores the mundane moments that make up the fabric of everyday life and Shanon's battle with structural racism in the quickly gentrifying Bay Area where the wealth gap is comparable to that of a developing country.
